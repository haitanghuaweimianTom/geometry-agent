# Few-shot Example: Plane geometry — tangent property (切线性质定理)

Problem: AB 切圆 O 于点 A, OA 是半径. 求证 OA ⊥ AB.
Goal: Prove OA ⊥ AB.

Reasoning trace (each claim is verified before use; computation goes through tools):
- Step 1: claim A lies on circle O (A 是切点, 在圆上).
  → call verify("On", "A", "C_O")
  ← {"verified":"true","evidence":"A is on circle O"}
- Step 2: claim OA is a radius (O 是圆心, A 在圆上 → OA 是半径).
  → call verify("Center", "O", "C_O")
  ← {"verified":"true","evidence":"O is center of C_O"}
- Step 3: compute the dot product OA·AB via coordinates to confirm perpendicularity (不手算).
  → call execute_code(code="import sympy as sp\nO=sp.Matrix([0,0]); A=sp.Matrix([3,0]); B=sp.Matrix([3,4])\nOA=A-O; AB=B-A\ndot=OA.dot(AB)\nprint('dot=', dot)")
  ← {"success":true,"output":"dot= 0","value":null}
- Step 4: dot=0 ⟹ OA ⊥ AB. By the tangent property theorem (切线垂直于过切点的半径), OA ⊥ AB.
